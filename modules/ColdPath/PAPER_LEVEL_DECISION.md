# ColdPath paper-level decision

**NO-GO under the current platform and authority.** Completed experiments
establish bounded single-host mechanisms: user-scoped prefix preparation can
overlap cold model loading, including adversarial early arrival, on two GGUF
sizes. They do not establish a reliable announced-lead controller (E024
performance confirmation No-Go), general total-system cost, cache displacement
or tenant-interference safety (E028 No-Go). This is not yet a CCF-B-defensible
systems contribution. Do not expand or market the line as a paper without new
authorized multi-tenant/pressure evidence and a controller mechanism that
passes a preregistered performance gate.

`RiskPrefetch` is recorded as a separate, inactive candidate, not a revival of
the rejected controller. E029 found that the only currently authorized public
trace lacks a non-oracle notice and model-state identity, so it cannot activate
this candidate. The paper-level NO-GO therefore remains in force.

E030 additionally closed the unmodified-runtime ArrivalSplit fallback. E031
found a real router queue/LRU capability, but not an admissible workload or
cost/harm evidence. Therefore all currently testable ColdPath variants remain
NO-GO as a paper mainline; the router result is technical feasibility only.
